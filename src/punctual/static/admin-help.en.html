Administrator manual

Accounts, daily checks, corrections and reports.

1. New accounts

  1. In Employees tap Generate link, copy the link and send it to your employees (chat or email). One link works for everyone, for 6 hours. A new link invalidates the old one.
  2. The employee creates their own account. It shows up in the list as inactive (dimmed row).
  3. Tap Edit, tick Active account, pick the daily norm, the role and the allowed sites, then Save. Without assigned sites the employee cannot clock in.
  4. You can also create an account directly, with a password you set (the Add employee form).

Password rule: at least 12 characters, one uppercase letter, one digit and one special character. The email cannot be changed after creation.

2. Passwords, lockouts, deactivation

  • Password reset: Edit, fill in New password. All the employee's sessions are closed and the account is unlocked.
  • Deactivation: untick Active account. The history stays; the employee can no longer sign in.
  • You cannot deactivate or demote yourself.
  • The first administrator is created from the command line: punctual-admin create-admin --name "Name" --email "email".

3. Sites and QR codes

In Sites you define each workplace (position and allowed radius). In QR codes you print each site's code and display it at the entrance. If a code reached someone it should not have, regenerate the site's key: the old code stops working.

4. Daily check

  • Dashboard: who is present, unclosed shifts and the days to review from the last 14 days. Mark a day as reviewed once you have checked it.
  • Rejected attempts: punches that were refused (no location, shift already open or closed, site not assigned), kept for 90 days. Punches made outside the zone or with weak GPS are not refused: they are recorded and appear under "To review" in the Dashboard, where you approve them.

5. Corrections and requests

  • Corrections: approve or reject employee requests, add a missing punch or edit a punch. A reason is required.
  • A punch is never deleted: it is voided. An edit voids the original and adds the replacement. Everything stays in Change history, for labour inspections.
  • Leave: approve or reject vacation, medical leave and free-day requests. The decision is final.

6. Reports

In Reports pick the period and the employee and export CSV. Public holidays, vacation, medical leave and free days are marked; weekends and holidays expect no hours.
